## 1. Original UI-Enabled Opportunities (Explicitly Separate From Approved Blueprint)

Every item below is **`Proposal — Newly Proposed`** (NOT in `PRODUCT-BLUEPRINT.md` and NOT approved for implementation without explicit owner decision):

| ID | Original Opportunity | Concrete Writer Problem Solved | Required New Capability / Mechanism | Alternatives & Tradeoffs | Feasibility & Risk | Candidate Timing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **UI-NEW-01** | **Universal Dual-Pane Companion Split (`max 2 panes`)** | Authors constantly need two artifacts side-by-side (*Ch. 6 + Leyla profile*, *Review Issue + Source Passage*, *Ending A + Alternative B*, *Import Source + Extracted Candidates*) without juggling overlapping modals or jumping across rooms. | Lightweight split-pane shell state (`primaryPane`, optional `companionPane`, draggable accessible `role="separator"` divider, mobile single-pane swap). | *Alternative:* Bespoke slide-over drawers per feature (higher inconsistency, weaker side-by-side comparison). *Tradeoff:* Adds pane-state persistence complexity; strictly cap at 2 panes to prevent IDE tab chaos. | **High feasibility / Low–Medium risk** (pure UI layout state) | `WP03` (Shell) |
| **UI-NEW-02** | **Interactive Chronology & Scene-State Playhead ("As-Of Chapter Scrubber")** | In mysteries and long novels, a static Story Map or Knowledge Matrix conflates Chapter 1 ignorance with Chapter 12 revelations, causing spoiler mistakes. | Scrubber control (`As of: Ch. 1 … Ch. N`) that filters Knowledge Matrix, Story Map edges, and Thread states strictly to evidence anchored `<= selected chapter` (reusing `E1` local index anchors). | *Alternative:* Separate manual snapshots per chapter (too tedious). *Tradeoff:* Only reflects facts with explicit scene anchors; undated facts must stay clearly labeled `Global / Unanchored`. | **Medium–High feasibility / Low risk** (builds directly on `WP02`/`WP04` local index) | `WP04` |
| **UI-NEW-03** | **Split-Jump Evidence Highlighting (Citation Hover/Click Peek)** | Clicking a source link (`Ch. 2 §2`) in Diagnostics, Story Q&A, or Import currently navigates the author completely away from their review context. | Clicking any citation opens the exact passage in the Companion Split Pane (or inline popover on mobile) with the verbatim span scrolled & highlighted in warm ochre — keeping Review/Q&A live beside it. | *Alternative:* Full navigation with back button (loses mental diff state). *Tradeoff:* Requires reliable span-to-scroll mapping (already improved by `WP01 M2` verbatim anchor resolution). | **High feasibility / Low risk** | `WP02` / `WP03` |
| **UI-NEW-04** | **Global Author Command Palette (`Cmd/Ctrl + K`) with Plain-English Intent Verbs** | Rich creative suites accumulate dozens of views and commands; authors forget where *Dual-Track Timeline*, *Save Alternative*, or *Backup Archive* live. | Local instant fuzzy search over projects, chapters, entities, sessions, AND plain-English verbs (*"who knows"*, *"flashback"*, *"protect lock"*, *"backup"*), zero AI required. | *Alternative:* Deep top-bar dropdown menus (high visual clutter, poor keyboard reach). *Tradeoff:* Must remain supplemental — never hide essential actions inside `Cmd+K` alone. | **High feasibility / Very Low risk** | `WP03` |
| **UI-NEW-05** | **Ambient Local "Scene Rhythm & Dialogue Density" Margin Bar (Zero AI)** | During drafting and pacing revision, writers struggle to spot 8-page exposition walls or uneven chapter lengths without reading an abstract stats dashboard. | Deterministic client-side paragraph/dialogue/word-count micro-sparkline along the `Write` chapter outline (100% local regex/heuristic, optional toggle, zero opaque "quality scores"). | *Alternative:* Numeric word-count column only (misses dialogue-vs-narration rhythm). *Tradeoff:* Dialogue quotation heuristics vary across languages (`"..."`, `«...»`, `—`); must stay strictly optional and transparent. | **High feasibility / Low risk** | `WP04` or `WP06` |
| **UI-NEW-06** | **Inline Word-Level Diff Scrubber with Per-Sentence Cherry-Pick** | When AI proposes a 250-word scene strengthening pass, authors often love 2 sentences and dislike 3 — today they face an all-or-nothing apply or tedious manual copy-paste. | Sentence/hunk checkboxes inside the Before/After diff sheet that deterministically recompile the exact `UnifiedProposal` patch before apply (reusing `E3` deterministic regeneration). | *Alternative:* Edit the whole proposed text blob manually (slower, harder to spot unintended drift). *Tradeoff:* Per-hunk selection must re-validate Hard Locks (`OD-06`) before enabling Apply. | **Medium feasibility / Medium risk** (requires hunk-level patch builder in `E3`) | `WP04` |
| **UI-NEW-07** | **Zero-AI "Reading Aloud Rhythm & Echo Highlighter" (Local Repetition Lens)** | Pairs naturally with `S-05` Compression & Redundancy Lens: writers often repeat crutch words or identical sentence openings unconsciously across a scene. | Deterministic local toggle inside `Write → Read` mode highlighting repeated n-grams / crutch words in nearby paragraphs (explicitly respecting deliberate literary repetition — never flagging them as "errors"). | *Alternative:* AI-only compression pass (costs tokens, slower). *Tradeoff:* Multilingual tokenization (Turkish agglutinative suffixes) needs care; label honestly as a local reading aid. | **High feasibility / Low risk** | `WP04` / `WP06` |
| **UI-NEW-08** | **Session Resume Breadcrumb Trail ("Where I Left Off")** | Returning novelists (`J-10`) often had 2–3 connected threads active (*Ch. 4 draft → Harbor Twists session → Brass Lens issue*) — a single "last tab" restore misses the full context. | Lightweight local-only recent activity trail (last 5 meaningful local milestones: edited scene, active Studio session, open review item) displayed on `Project Overview` (`GS-05` / `IR-05`), strictly zero AI calls. | *Alternative:* Reopen only the last active screen. *Tradeoff:* Must respect privacy/local-only storage and clear cleanly when a project is deleted. | **High feasibility / Very Low risk** | `WP03` / `WP06` |

> **Deliberately Rejected Novelty Ideas (Selectivity Check — `RESEARCH-ASSIGNMENT.md §8`):**
> - *Gamified daily word streaks / "Story Quality 84/100" gauges:* Rejected — violates `PRODUCT-BLUEPRINT.md §2` (*no opaque scores or artificial completion percentages*) and infantilizes serious literary writers.
> - *Always-on floating AI chat bubble in the corner of `Write`:* Rejected — invites chat-wrapper sprawl and intrudes on prose focus.
> - *Multi-tab IDE window tiling (`3+` arbitrary split panes):* Rejected — turns a calm literary workspace into a cockpit; max 2 panes (`UI-NEW-01`) delivers 95% of the utility with a fraction of the cognitive cost.

## 2. Strategic Sequencing: Functional-First vs. UI-First vs. Staged Evolution

`RESEARCH-ASSIGNMENT.md §8` asks for a direct strategic comparison of delivery paths — **without pausing or superseding active `WP01`**:

| Strategy | Description | Advantages | Risks & Migration / Rework Costs | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Strategy 1: Pure Functional-First (Defer ALL UI until `WP06`)** | Ship `WP01`–`WP05` inside the current `AdaptivePlanWorkspace` + `398px` right rail, then do one massive visual/IA overhaul at the end. | Zero early layout refactoring. | **High rework & unusable mid-journey:** Trying to jam Creative Studio (`WP03`), Knowledge Matrices (`WP04`), Dual-Track Timelines (`WP04`), and Multiverse Comparison (`WP05`) into today's `398px` rail and `28%` graph canvas guarantees either throwaway UI code or broken author workflows for months. | **Reject** |
| **Strategy 2: Pure UI-First Big-Bang Redesign (Pause `WP01`, rebuild entire UI & design system now)** | Freeze functional reliability work, rewrite every component and CSS token first, then resume `WP01`–`WP05`. | Immediate visual beauty. | **Violates `OD-03` & delays trust:** Pretty surfaces over broken OAuth refresh, dirty-draft locks, or malformed imports erode writer trust; designing complex controls before `E1/E2/E3` data contracts stabilize causes wasted mock UI. | **Reject** |
| **Strategy 3 (Recommended): Staged IA-Skeleton + Targeted Early Fixes + Dedicated Design-System Convergence** | **(a) During `WP01` (no pause):** Ship already-scoped `WP01` trust/settings/recovery UX PLUS tiny low-risk surgical fixes for confirmed severe defects (`UX-01` dead inspector button, `UX-03` safe formatted preview if feasible, `UX-08` missing focus ring) without a full shell rewrite.<br>**(b) During `WP02`:** Keep minimal usable surfaces for `Context Inspector`, local search, and `UnifiedProposal` diff preview.<br>**(c) At `WP03` Shell Boundary (after Comparative UI Study & Owner Decision `UID-01`–`UID-08`):** Land the **Clean Structural IA Shell** (1 slim bar, full-canvas rooms, optional Dual-Pane Split, unified semantic tokens retiring hijacked `--color-blue-*`, mobile navigation fix `UX-05`) ONCE so `WP03`, `WP04`, and `WP05` build directly into their permanent full-width homes.<br>**(d) At `WP06`:** Full visual craft polish, micro-typography refinement, overview/recovery integration, and cross-workspace accessibility audit. | Zero interruption to `WP01`; eliminates throwaway narrow-rail UI for `WP03`–`WP05`; anchors the design-system migration cleanly at `WP03` right after Owner UI sign-off. | Requires disciplined separation between *structural IA skeleton* (`WP03`) and *deep visual craft polish* (`WP06`), plus early token hygiene so `WP02`–`WP03` don't add a 5th styling dialect. | **Recommended** |
