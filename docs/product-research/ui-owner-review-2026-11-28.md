## 1. Owner observations (screenshots and actual use)
The owner supplied seventeen screenshots of the existing app, including Write, Import, AI Settings, Review and Plan/Inspector states. The owner reports difficulty understanding the purpose of tools, navigation and terminology despite earlier improvements. The following is a textual record; original screenshots have not been uploaded to GitHub. Do not treat the entire product as tested.
- Write: Edit and Preview look essentially identical; Markdown syntax remains literal. AI Writing Assistant and style tools are far below the editor, in collapsible panels; selection-aware wording and offsets are not useful to an ordinary author. Section metadata fields are extensive and poorly explained. Provide visible contextual entry points, natural-language examples and accessible help. Preserve source text and selection/undo/IME.
- Plan: large central graph dominates even when user needs to read or edit another complex view. Inbox opens left panel; Context toggles right inspector without making its purpose clear; Story Bible and Opportunities open tabs of same narrow inspector, Read Output controls bottom panel. More Views hides Timeline and Mind Map. Duplicate controls and nested tabs confuse the owner. Some fields (e.g. Story Seed) cannot readily expand; Story Bible multiline fields can be resized. Allow sensible auto-grow, manual resize, expand/full view and readable long content, not arbitrary universal resizers.
- Story Bible: group/filter ideas are useful, but Arc & Voice, World & Atmosphere, Canon & Exclusions, Unset and other technical labels lack approachable explanations; visuals distract from story work. Owner wants natural labels and examples, clearer organization and room to edit.
- Revision History: full three-column comparison squeezed into inspector, clipping/overlap. Export and backup controls appear within same History area. Timeline and Mind Map deserve full useful visual surfaces, not tiny lists/cards. Ideas/Opportunities should not be cramped in Plan.
- Review: technically dense, unclear distinction among readiness reminders, actual issues, local checks, semantic analysis, sync, evidence and audit. Explain what to do and why, keep advanced evidence accessible.
- AI Settings: excessively long vertically stacked provider/configuration/override controls, manual connection refresh followed by manual catalog refresh. Managed Gemini-only options seen in GAIS screenshots may reflect runtime availability, not proof other providers globally unsupported. Need clearer model/connection eligibility and one coherent connection completion flow.
- Import, Projects and About are broadly acceptable to owner; investigate improvements without gratuitous redesign. Image generation remains, secondary to story work; no new visual generation work now; future story-linked chapter/scene/character illustrations possible.
