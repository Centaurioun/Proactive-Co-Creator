## 1. Product purpose and research boundary

PCC evolved from an interactive Google AI Studio creative prompt/story application. The original attraction was expanding a small seed into unexpected ideas, visual entities, relationships and a branching creative space. Additional goals: faithfully reconstruct existing imported narratives; identify characters, relationships, events, chronology, threads and inconsistencies; support iterative manuscript development and revision; offer author-controlled creative partnership rather than merely generate text. The graph and creative discovery must remain prominent. Avoid software-engineering terminology in normal writing journeys. Primary value: real functioning creative operations, not number of tests or features. Image generation and CI/toolchain modernization are secondary to the creative product research, not erased requirements.

The next Google AI Studio assignment is a comprehensive documentation-only product discovery, current-state investigation, UX analysis, brainstorming and evolution study. No source, dependency, schema, config, test or build changes; no auto-fix. Inspect actual sandbox behavior with isolated synthetic projects and available managed provider within bounded consent. Distinguish observed results, source-confirmed behavior, hypotheses, restrictions and proposals. Discover unknown defects, suggest removals/consolidation, challenge assumptions, propose alternatives and examples, assess user value/dependencies/risk, and recommend an integrated roadmap. ## 2. Owner-endorsed workspace directions (CE-01)

Separate Creative Studio (discover possibilities and introduce original ideas) from Story Development (expand and strengthen the existing narrative, preserving its intended identity). Keep distinct user-facing workspaces with explanatory subtitles, not cramped inspector panels. Write, Plan, Review and project management remain relevant; exact global navigation is researchable. Avoid unnecessary duplicate subsystems. Creative Studio modes open dedicated spacious views:
- Discover with AI: AI-led story-specific creative opportunities.
- Guided Exploration: adaptive questions/options based on prior choices.
- Focused Development: author chooses scope and objective, via selected characters/events/relationships/objects/locations, graph nodes, manuscript passage, scene, chapter or whole story; support multiple selections and free text.
- Story Lab: what-if, alternative story directions, experimental branches, surprising links and endings.
- Collaborative Exploration is the ability to move seamlessly between AI-led and author-led directions; assistant recommendation: shared interaction pattern rather than mandatory fifth screen.
- Saved Ideas, Exploration History, Active Sessions and Selected Changes; access and resume previous work.
Story Development candidates: Expand Story, Develop Chapters, Character Depth, Plot Development, Scene & Dialogue, Pacing & Structure, Voice & Style, Continue Story, Narrative Compression. Example: expand a three-page story without unwanted new twists or changing core plot. Preservation Policy: user-selectable core plot, characters, ending, voice, chronology, relationships, genre/tone. Creative Studio and Story Development may exchange drafts but have distinct primary intentions.

## 3. Creative opportunity actions (CE-02)

Owner endorses Preview, Accept/Apply, Edit, Regenerate, Develop Further, Save for Later, Dismiss and Undo. No redundant Accept -> Send to Change Review -> extra technical screen once concrete changes have been reviewed. Distinguish accepting a non-mutating idea into collection from applying concrete changes. Regenerate is per-card, not entire collection; keep other ideas, prior alternative lineage and optional user guidance. Develop Further preserves the essence of a promising idea and creates refinements rather than replacing it. 
- Display AI-suggested directions (e.g. more mysterious, scientific, emotional, complex, original, unexpected, Surprise Me); multiselect and own direction.
- Suggest More Directions appends additional distinct options without losing previous selections.
- Write your own direction input has two separate AI actions: Suggest Directions produces new possible directions grounded in the input, not a concrete idea; Generate Idea produces a concrete creative idea from the input. Develop with Selected Directions combines chosen directions into an evolved version.
- Manual editing of the idea and actual proposed changes; distinguish description edits from patch edits.
- Allow combine ideas, individual alternative history, comparison, optional notes; don't silently overwrite useful previous outputs.
- Save for Later explicitly bookmarks; Dismiss hides/rejects with recoverable history where useful; Undo/revert applied changes without erasing unrelated later edits, flag dependencies/conflicts.
- Selection, liking, saved ideas and draft revisions never equal application. All selections, typed instructions, AI results, questions and answers are recoverable drafts; only explicit final author action mutates canonical narrative.
- Prefer visible status and simple user-facing labels; exact UI and technical schema remain research questions.

## 4. Creative Session and shared proposal engine (CE-03)

Persistent project-scoped sessions: scope, original idea, goals, constraints, chosen directions, author inputs, alternatives/versions, parent links, merges of ideas, questions/answers, saved/dismissed choices, draft patches and reviewed revision. Autosave on local edits and valid results; restore after reload/project switching; show actual save status and preserve last durable draft on failure. Do not silently resume provider calls. Distinguish autosave, save/bookmark, selection, acceptance, application and archive. Sessions can be paused/resumed; avoid forced multi-step wizard; Skip, Back, Generate Now, Finish, Save Draft, Continue Exploring. No provider call required for every selection; explicitly trigger bounded meaningful generation. Maintain canonical project data, source evidence, author approval, CAS/revision and stale-response protections. Use common proposal mechanics for creative ideas, diagnostics fixes, writing assistance, sync, Decision Inbox and natural-language edits while keeping actual problems separate from optional creativity. Shared Narrative Intelligence: story understanding, context intelligence, creative exploration, narrative relations, proposal management, impact analysis, branch/version control, author preferences. Shared manuscript, Story Bible, graph, evidence, revision history, sessions; no competing authoritative registries.

## 5. Final Preview, change map and impact (CE-04)

Owner wants per-idea and final aggregate Preview before applying, with Edit, Save Draft, Apply All Changes and ability to select specific changes. Preview should show actual before/after manuscript ranges, model fields, entities, relationships, Story Bible, added/changed/removed elements, unchanged protected elements. Assistant proposed Summary, Before/After, Change Map, Impact Analysis views. Change Map: visual graph (Mermaid-like, not necessarily Mermaid or a tree), Before / After / Changes only; preserve node positions; edges with direction/predicate; added/changed/removed/unchanged labels and visual styles; click through to relevant text and proposal. Derived from actual proposed patch and exact base revision, not independently invented AI diagrams. Distinguish concrete changes from potential downstream effects, explicitly identify uncertainty and lack of proposed patch. Impact analysis can identify other affected chapters, characters, relationships, plot, continuity, evidence. Edit or selection change requires refreshed coherent preview; detect dependency conflicts; apply coherent selected transaction only, do not silently include excluded edits. Review stale base before apply. Reversible history and safe selective revert. Do not reintroduce complex mandatory extra review journey.

## 6. Alternative projects and Story Multiverse (CE-05)

Owner explicitly endorses final choices: Apply to Current Project; Save as Alternative Project; Save as Draft; Continue Exploring. Alternative project applies reviewed changes to independent copy with distinct identity, manuscript/model/Bible and revision history; original unchanged. Save alternative NEVER automatically switches current project. After durable creation, report success and offer Continue Current Project (default) or Open Alternative (explicit). Preserve origin project, base revision and source exploration lineage; independent edits do not propagate. Story Multiverse shows optional ancestry/branch tree of alternative projects. Compare Alternative Stories: qualitative comparison of plot, atmosphere, tension, creative strengths/weaknesses and tradeoffs, not invented quality scores. Combine ideas or selectively transfer from alternatives through preview and conflict-aware proposal; no implicit project merge. Research distinctions between temporary sandbox branches, idea versions and actual independent projects, safe clone/ID remapping and lineage when parent removed.

## 7. Preferences and protected elements (CE-06)

Creative Compass: editable summary of current creative directions, context carried across the session, offer next directions. Avoid invented precision; diagram is preference visualization, not story quality score. Creative DNA: optional user-saved longer-term project/general preferences; Follow My Preferences / Challenge My Preferences / Blend Both; avoid repetitive filter bubble. Creative Intensity: Subtle / Balanced / Bold / Radical as author risk preference, not technical model reasoning or temperature. Creative Constraints: per-session instructions such as preserve ending/no new characters/scientific plausibility. Creative Lock (not luck): protected narrative items, AI can recommend but author also wants to freely type additional items, with + Add another and repeatable rows. Support selecting exact entities, relations, passages, events, ending, world rules; custom natural-language locks need clarification/grounding to structured target where possible. Lock enforcement and conflicts should be visible; no silent override. Scope project or session; distinguish hard lock from soft preference; user may explicitly unlock or explore an alternative under separate constraints. Unstructured instruction must not pretend to have deterministic enforceability. Exact design is researchable.

## 8. Endorsed creative capabilities catalog (CE-07)

All below endorsed for research and eventual consideration, not necessarily independent features/tabs. Consolidate related capabilities through shared engine:
- Connection Discovery / hidden connections: select several seemingly unrelated events, objects, characters, locations, relationships; generate plausible, surprising causal, temporal, psychological, scientific, mystical, symbolic or other links. Example: stopped watch 03:17 in Ch1 and unexplained radio signal in Ch7; offer multiple directions, combine selected directions and add author's constraints. Ground existing events in source; new links remain hypotheses.
- Story Seeds / Discover Hidden Story Seeds: find small existing details with unrealized creative potential; turn into substantial plots or clues before adding arbitrary new objects. Foreshadowing Architect: setup, reminders, misdirection, payoff; forward from early clue or reverse from intended ending; source-linked and subtle. Mystery Builder, Plot Promise Tracker, Unresolved Story Threads, Open Questions Board.
- Character Intelligence, Hidden Motivation, Character Interview (fictional roleplay labeled exploratory), Alternative Perspectives/Perspective Switcher; separate what character knows, suspects, falsely believes, or has not learned, scoped to scene/time. Character Knowledge Map, Reader's Perspective / Reader Knowledge Timeline / Reader Notes: distinguish narrative order and story chronology, reader vs character knowledge, spoiler boundaries, deliberate uncertainty. Review possible contradictions without falsely declaring them.
- Creative Challenge / Challenge My Idea: expose cliches, weak causal links, contrivance, unintended consequences, propose alternatives; constructive critique, keep original if desired; avoid automatic endorsement and aesthetic quality scores.
- What Happens Next?, Scene Simulator, Creative Crossroads, Alternative Endings Lab, What-if story branches, Reverse Plotting / Reverse Engineering a Twist, Butterfly Effect / Narrative Ripple Effect / Narrative Impact Map / change simulation.
- Combine Ideas, Surprise Me, Creative Cross-Pollination (science, mythology, psychology etc. as inspiration, distinguish fiction from factual claims), Theme Weaver, Narrative Tension Map, Idea Incubator, Creative Continuation, Creative Brief (editable summary of goals/decisions), Idea Marketplace (private searchable saved idea collection, not external marketplace), Merge Alternatives (selective transfers, no automatic merge).
- Story Questions / talk to the story: answer grounded questions with supporting passage and uncertainty; identify unresolved threads and forgotten characters.
- Simple and advanced working experiences, contextual actions, flexible navigation, preserve discoverability. Proactive assistant: non-interrupting optional suggestions during writing was proposed earlier; not explicitly approved for autonomous provider calls. Research opt-in, budget and manual mode first.

## 9. New owner request: Grill Me / creative questioning (CE-08)

User wants a voluntarily invoked adaptive interview at project inception or ANY scoped point in a story when stuck or developing a small idea. AI asks probing questions, challenges assumptions, offers directions; author answers, can continue as long as desired, stop at any time and see an editable preview concept derived from responses. Do not force this workflow on users; ordinary direct generation remains. Questions may lead to new ideas, not merely fill forms. Save question/answer history and unresolved points in session; do not apply automatically.

External research source: mattpocock/skills main commit bbadaa631c37af594e5a05c9cefacd9956136b18, skills/productivity/README.md and seven SKILL.md files. Source findings: grill-me is a user-invoked wrapper for grilling, not removed in inspected revision. Grilling maps dependency tree and asks currently answerable independent decisions in rounds with recommended answers, recomputes frontier on replies; facts researched by agent rather than asking user; original stops at resolved tree and confirmation. PCC adaptation (proposal): default one high-value question or optional small independent batch, answer options with reasoned recommendation, free-text, multi-choice where valid, let AI propose, skip/defer/keep mystery, change earlier answer and invalidate dependent draft decisions for re-review, pause/resume, preview now; no forced full resolution. Distinguish confirmed facts, tentative decisions, unknowns, deliberate mystery and creative proposals. No hidden provider call loop. Allow intensity (gentle exploration vs critical challenge), scope, exit/preview after any round. Integrate as named Grill Me / Ask Me Questions action accessible from seed onboarding, Studio modes, selected manuscript/graph, Story Development, individual idea and Review; shared session rather than another global tab. This is design research, not implementation approval.

## 10. Other productivity skills and adaptation hypotheses (CE-09)

- handoff: source compacts conversation into temporary handoff, references existing artifacts, suggests skills, redacts secrets. PCC proposed Session Brief / resume elsewhere; record goal, decisions, questions, selected versions, current scope, unresolved choices and references; export only explicitly chosen content, no credentials; link to existing records rather than duplicating.
- to-questionnaire: source targets a knowledge gap in another person, one question per decision, important-first and partial/unknown answers. PCC proposed asynchronous author questionnaire or collaborator/beta-reader feedback sheet, export/import as draft responses, no automatic acceptance; focus on author-only creative decisions. Not a mandatory interrogation.
- wait-what: source re-pitches misunderstood message using plain language and project glossary. PCC proposed Explain Differently / clarify this idea, concrete example, why this matters, what would change, no new canonical facts.
- teach: source stateful learning mission, lessons, references, learning records, feedback loop, resources, appropriate challenge. PCC proposed optional Writing Coach / Explain the Technique, contextual lessons about pacing, foreshadowing, POV etc., separate from story facts and diagnostics; no mandatory course or unsupported claims.
- writing-for-agents: source progressive disclosure, meaningful trigger pointers, co-location, one source of truth and checkable completion criteria. PCC proposed context assembly discipline, stable glossary/story references, operation-specific retrieval, precise prompt contracts and layered UI; not a separate user-facing feature by default. 
- grill-me and grilling are complementary invocation/mechanism, not two competing product features. Read all seven main skill definitions and category README; bundled supporting lesson formats and skill mechanics not exhaustively analyzed in this pass. ## 11. Six-cycle synthesis and open decisions

1. Purpose: original visual creative partnership, faithful narrative understanding and controlled development.
2. Discovery: reported core AI failures, confusing change review, cramped panel, lost idea risks, creative questioning need; future study verify actual current behavior.
3. Divergence: expanded panel versus dedicated workspaces versus wholesale redesign; prefer separate Studio and Development with common engine; alternative workflows remain researchable.
4. Critical review: avoid forced wizard, uncontrolled AI requests, feature sprawl, duplicative authority, artificial creative scores, silent truncation, overwrites, stale previews, contradictions, overcomplicated plot, fabricated evidence, accidental canonization, accidental project switch.
5. Refinement: append new directions, two own-direction actions, autosaved sessions, explicit staged selection, concrete diff/graph vs speculative impacts, custom locks, reversible aggregate application, independent alternative copies; Grill Me voluntary and interruptible.
6. Integration: shared narrative intelligence and session/proposal engine, distinct UX per creative intent, preserve import fidelity, privacy, persistence, user agency; prioritize real user value and verify actual journeys in future work.

Open research decisions: exact navigation and consolidation; question depth, modes and batching; session lifecycle, storage/retention and recovery; model/provider contracts and budgets; qualitative preference representation; enforceability of free-text locks; graph projection and dependency handling; alternative project cloning and selective merge; proactive suggestions opt-in; sequencing and scope. The owner endorsed the ideas through this conversation, not their precise implementation or all research hypotheses. No code change or merge authorized by this document.
