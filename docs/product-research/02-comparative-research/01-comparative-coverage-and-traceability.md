## 1. Evidence & Disposition Legend

- **Evidence Tags:**
  - `Live` — Observed in `http://localhost:3000` preview (`2026-11-28`) or bounded managed-Gemini synthetic probes.
  - `Source` — Directly confirmed in current workspace source files.
  - `Source-Reassessed` — Re-checked directly against source during `PCC-RESEARCH-02`, sharpening or correcting a `PCC-RESEARCH-01` causal claim.
  - `Owner-Endorsed` — Explicitly endorsed direction in `owner-brainstorming-2026-11-28.md` (research input, not technical spec approval).
  - `Hypothesis` — Inferred causal mechanism or behavioral expectation requiring empirical verification.
  - `Exploratory` — Creative or architectural concept requiring prototype/user validation.
- **Research Dispositions:** `Adopt` (retain substantively as framed) · `Refine` (keep core need, sharpen design/cause/safeguards) · `Combine` (merge into a stronger unified engine or workflow while preserving distinct user-facing intents where needed) · `Simplify` (achieve the need with lower UX/architectural ceremony) · `Investigate` (unresolved empirical/technical question needing targeted verification before committing) · `Defer` (valid lower-priority or high-cost idea held for later phase) · `Reject` (fails critical review; explicit rationale provided).
