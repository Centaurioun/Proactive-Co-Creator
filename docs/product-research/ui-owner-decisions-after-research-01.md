The comparative study may challenge details with evidence, but may not silently reverse approved needs. All proposed names, exact control placement and layout specifics are refinable.

## A. Accepted decisions UID-01–UID-08
- UID-01: optional shared Dual-Pane Companion, adjustable width and saved layout state; single-pane default and constrained-width swap/full views. Not two mandatory panes; cap concurrent major content surfaces at two by default. A binder or slim contextual affordance is not another full workbench. Support useful pairings (manuscript + character, review + source, original + alternative, import + extraction, studio + map); explicit active/focused target and project identity. Comparison of two projects defaults to read-only reference on at least the secondary side; never route mutations by whichever project happens to be active.
- UID-02: keep Creative Studio and Story Development as distinct, readily reachable author-intent workspaces; keep Review distinct from development. Desktop and mobile navigation may present them differently but must not collapse their intentions to solve space constraints. Projects, Write, World, Studio, Develop, Review remain the accepted structural direction. Exact labels and mobile presentation remain refinable.
- UID-03: final visual identity remains undecided until comparative research and concrete comparable visual concepts are reviewed. Explore Warm Literary Press, Quiet Ink & Monograph, Nocturnal Desk Lamp, Cartographer's Folio and stronger new alternatives. A warm hybrid is a candidate, not an owner-selected palette.
- UID-04: correct confirmed functional/accessibility defects at earliest appropriate reviewed milestone; safe formatted Markdown, non-working destinations, keyboard focus, silent hidden list entries, and basic mobile reachability should not wait for aesthetic overhaul. Do not interrupt an in-flight WP01 or silently expand its scope. Track residual repairs after its review.
- UID-05: plain author-centered labels and useful examples. Keep technical accuracy, epistemic distinctions, actual revision identity, provenance and advanced evidence reachable. Consistent names, no sample-story jargon in generic controls.
- UID-06: retain all eight new opportunities with refinements below. Consolidate into shared systems rather than eight independent menus.
- UID-07: AI Settings (connections/model defaults/overrides/embeddings/usage) and project backup/recovery separate, cross-linked. Preserve local-only defaults, explicit consent, truthful availability and actual storage/inference separation.
- UID-08: adaptive first/returning launch; user can choose direct last-work resume or meaningful overview. Empty/seed/imported project gets appropriate next steps, never an obligatory sample/wizard. No AI request merely to reopen.

## C. Six further ChatGPT ideas accepted by owner
- UIRF-01 Workspace Presets: save/restore named user-defined layout preferences, e.g. Writing, Creative Exploration, Story Research, Revision. Persist scoped view layout, open panes, width, selection as appropriate, not an implicit copy or rollback of story state. Built-in useful defaults; optional complexity; reset safely if destination removed or screen too small.
- UIRF-02 Pinned Story Elements: optional lightweight pinning of relevant character, relationship, rule, clue or source while navigating. Pins are project-scoped references, not duplicate canonical data or permanently open cards; preserve stale/deleted/changed status and clear controls. Do not create an always-present third workbench.
- UIRF-03 Cross-Workspace Deep Links: stable scoped links between character, scene, source, creative session, issue and relevant views. Preserve previous navigation/scroll/cursor/filter state; distinguish open here, open companion, return; handle stale IDs and unsaved work. No automatic project switch/mutation.
- UIRF-04 Show Me How: optional concise story-specific examples and contextual explanations, static synthetic examples clearly identified; not a forced tutorial or automatic AI action. Include visible action-oriented empty states, accessible help; avoid tooltip-only teaching.
- UIRF-05 Expand Any Major View: primary full-width destination for major work (Story Bible, Timeline, Mind Map, History, Preview, Studio etc.) plus expand/collapse and sensible resize. Return to previous context. Avoid arbitrary nested modals or resizers on all controls.
- UIRF-06 Distraction-Free Reading: formatted manuscript reading, typography/zoom preferences, no intrusive metadata/chrome; select passage and explicitly request improve, ask, note or explore without changing source. Support true Edit/Preview, consider Split and continuous reading as appropriate; raw HTML/script and unexpected remote resources remain blocked.

## D. Critical review corrections to the independent study
1. A genuine associative Mind Map and an epistemic Knowledge Matrix solve different questions; neither replaces the other. Story Map/relationship network, timeline, associative map, knowledge matrix, causal/dependency graph, exact patch Change Map and speculative Impact Analysis have different semantics and need clear labels/accessibility. Consolidate infrastructure, not meaning.
2. Story Development remains separate from Review and Creative Studio, including on mobile. Do not merge distinct author intentions to fit six destinations.
3. Report's 48px single bar, >=70% canvas, 40/44px target sizes and thresholds are design targets to evaluate, not absolute constraints on every viewport or every state. Prefer measurable task reachability, readable zoom, actual available canvas and comfort. Distinguish WCAG requirement from voluntary stricter ergonomic target; don't claim compliance based on wireframes. Respect actual browser/native behavior, text expansion, keyboard, screen readers and reduced motion.
4. Independently recomputed WCAG relative luminance ratios: proposed --border-strong #B8B1A8 on #FFFDF9 = approximately 2.09:1; #524B57 on #211E23 = approximately 1.96:1. Both fail report's claimed >=3:1 interactive outline target. #555059 on #FFFDF9 ~7.72:1. Correct the design proposal and verify actual foreground/background pairs and states, not just isolated swatches; decorative separators need not all meet interactive contrast criteria. No formal audit certificate.
5. Two-project comparison, selected-text actions, session resume and cross-workspace navigation require explicit identity/scope/revision and correct write target; never let visual convenience weaken durable saves, proposal CAS/locks, stale rejection, undo, private data or provider consent.
6. Independent report's ~step counts and projected gains are illustrative unless backed by measured equivalent task traces. Existing Writing Assistant in Write must be represented accurately; do not assume every selected-text action currently requires Plan navigation. Re-evaluate disputed source observations and separate actual vs projected journeys. Preserve original report unchanged.
7. The first study has reported preview observations but no independently repeated live measurement in ChatGPT review; no user study, formal accessibility certification or large-novel performance qualification. Research claims remain appropriately bounded.
