## 2. Product purpose, vision, and scope
PCC is a writer-focused local-first AI narrative workspace: transform a seed idea or imported story into an editable working manuscript plus canonical semantic narrative model, with evidence and author control. Not just a prompt generator, not a Word clone, not a team/cloud platform, not a video generator. Story primary, Image secondary. Write / Plan / Review are flexible modes, not a mandatory wizard.
- Write: outline, chapters/scenes, editor, versioned manuscript, style guidance, selected-range AI draft proposals, explicit adoption, local editing without AI.
- Plan: directed Belief Graph, stable entities/attributes/relations, Story Bible, Decision Inbox, explicit accept/defer/reject, natural-language batch diff and explicit apply, context inspector, timeline/mind map/relationship views, prompt refinement and explicit generation.
- Review: local consistency checks and readiness, scoped semantic diagnostics, fix alternatives, bidirectional manuscript/model synchronization, evidence & audit, optional opportunities, history and output shortcuts.
- Project hub: create/switch/delete, import and preview, source/evidence review, export content, backup/restore preview and restore-as-copy, undo/redo, revisions. Do not remove existing features to simplify the UI. New/unsupported states must honestly disclose limits. Preserve the writer's original source, edits, and decisions.
- Four distinct authorities: immutable source (original bytes by default when safely supported, extracted-only disclosed); editable Working Manuscript; canonical accepted semantic model; immutable generated artifacts, adopted only through explicit author action. Shared evidence/audit registry records spans, version/digest, provenance, decisions and short evidence-based rationale. Beliefs, quotes, hypotheses, inferences and confirmed world facts differ; pending AI output is not canon. Document order is not story chronology. Directed multigraph supports parallel predicates and reverse edges. Source identity not name-only. No fabricated evidence, citations, or AI output labeled real when mocked.
- Product scope approved during Nov24 brainstorming rounds. Historical five-phase plan is retained, but the current owner wants finish/repair, not more broad development. Earlier Google AI Studio first, large vertical slices, and implementation-first/verification-last preferences applied to original implementation stage; current Nov27 repair decision supersedes those for this closeout: one issue at a time, meaningful regression test, targeted validation and independent review, full qualification at the end.
